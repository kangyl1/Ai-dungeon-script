## Chronology cross-check, not light-novel certification

The author's [web-novel in-world biography](https://ncode.syosetu.com/n9669bk/284/) places the later Orsted battle and Ariel's campaign in 425, the later Shirone war in 427, a Millis visit in 429 and Biheiril in 430. It is both a different edition and a fictional historical account, so its explanations of motives should not be treated as omniscient truth. We retain broad windows, not forced dates.

The [Japanese chronology overview](https://philo963.hateblo.jp/entry/mushoku-tensei-timeline) explicitly distinguishes inferred years and differing media. It was a discovery lead to the author's page, not the authority for a complete LN timeline. The [community timeline](https://mushokutensei.fandom.com/wiki/Timeline) mixes editions; full access failed during research.

## Deck corrections and remaining uncertainty

Fourteen entries differ from the supplied original: Calendar, Quagmire, Academy, Begaritt, Orsted Duel, Household, Children, Rudeus, Roxy, Sylphiette, Zenith, Orsted, Aisha, Norn. The attached change log preserves exact before/after text. Known conflicting years were removed or made provisional rather than replaced by unsupported precision. Other characterization and lore retained in those entries were not comprehensively verified.

The 17 selector records are broad reference periods, not exhaustive scene summaries. Detailed causes, exact dates and all alternative-route effects still need fuller source coverage. Original expansion material remains in the supplied deck. This release does not certify all 887 cards or claim that outside research will never be needed again.
